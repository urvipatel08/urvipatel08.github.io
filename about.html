Profile

I work where design thinking meets spatial analysis, to support resilient, people-centred cities.

I'm completing a Master of Urban Design in the Future Cities program at Thomas Jefferson University, graduating in December 2026. My work focuses on walkable environments, public realm design, and data-informed planning, combining architectural training with GIS-based urban research.

I have professional experience supporting architectural and planning teams through site analysis, zoning research, land development documentation, and design visualisation. I enjoy turning complex urban data into clear graphics, master plans, and presentation materials that guide collaborative decision-making.

My academic and professional projects explore pedestrian-oriented streets, mixed-use redevelopment, and climate-responsive urban design. I'm particularly interested in work that strengthens community life, improves accessibility, and enhances urban sustainability.
