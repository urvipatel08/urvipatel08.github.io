Introduction

I'm interested in the space between buildings.

I'm Urvi, an urban designer and architect finishing a Master of Urban Design at Thomas Jefferson University. I trained as an architect in Ahmedabad, and these days I spend most of my time on streets, stations, and the walking routes that connect them.

My work asks how design and data can make everyday places work better for the people who use them: how safe a street feels to cross, whether a station is easy to reach on foot, and who a plan actually serves. I use GIS to find the patterns and design to respond to them.

Welcome to the portfolio. Short on time? Start with my thesis. Otherwise, wander through the urban design studios, the GIS work, my research writing, and the architecture projects below.

Master's Thesis · Presented at ACSA 2026

Extending the Node-Place Model for TOD

Master of Urban Design, Future Cities · Thomas Jefferson University · 2026

Why do some rail stations with strong transit service see little development? A four-dimension GIS model scores all 50 of Philadelphia's subway and elevated stations on transit, land use, walkability, and social equity, then carries the weakest into site-specific design.

  • 50 stations on the Broad Street and Market-Frankford Lines
  • 20 variables across node, place, pedestrian, and social dimensions
  • Walnut-Locust leads at 0.68; Allegheny trails at 0.34
